1. PURPOSE AND FIXED SCOPE

Determine whether K's observed advantage over O transfers to a coding workload and appears under two training seeds. Run the already-qualified O and K algorithms. This experiment validates an existing component; it introduces no controller, correction, new loss, learning-rate search or additional training arm.

Use training seeds 17 and 29. For each seed, start both O and K from the same pinned released draft with fresh Adam, then complete four events of 128 responses. This is a controlled stream of separate coding requests with 128-response updates. It is not a multi-turn/domain-shift test, a one-update-per-turn test, or a convergence claim. Preserve valid negative or mixed results and stop at the fixed endpoint.
