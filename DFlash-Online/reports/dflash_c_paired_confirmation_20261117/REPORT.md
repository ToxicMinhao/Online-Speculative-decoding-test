## Why time admission stopped

The forecast policy was declared before model work: observed whole-task costs with a 1.3 multiplier, 600 seconds fixed contingency and 300 seconds for future CPU derivation/reconstruction work. It is a planning estimate, not a guaranteed upper bound. The inherited 1.5× + 600 forecast was disclosed separately and was not the adopted handoff requirement. Neither policy was changed after seeing qualification.

| Quantity at production admission | Seconds | Minutes |
|---|---:|---:|
| Elapsed already consumed |1,393.916|23.232|
| Raw remaining forecast, including CPU allowance |9,702.483|161.708|
| Remaining forecast with declared contingency |13,213.228|220.220|
| Total elapsed forecast |14,607.144|243.452|
| Hard elapsed cap |14,400.000|240.000|
| **Forecast shortfall** |**207.144**|**3.452**|

The corresponding total occupied-card forecast was 16,342.553 seconds (272.376 card-minutes), below the 300 card-minute limit. Disk and numerical checks passed. This is **an elapsed-forecast admission failure**, not an actual four-hour overrun, VRAM failure, algorithm failure or performance result.

Remaining training was repriced as the larger of its historical whole-task cost and `loading + startup + 128 * slowest measured pair + 45 seconds`. Parallel elapsed phases use their maximum task cost; occupied-card phases use their sum. Qualification's already-consumed work is included through the clock once. Future production has its own eight loads/startups. The 300-second future CPU allowance is added once inside the 1.3 multiplier: `actual elapsed + 1.3 × (grouped remaining work + 300) + 600`. Independent audits check this arithmetic against the retained records.

The raw remaining estimate is approximately 2 hours 42 minutes; the declared contingent estimate was 3 hours 40 minutes at the stop, while only 3 hours 37 minutes remained. Completing the full campaign under the unchanged admission rule was therefore not authorized. No alternative timing assumption or shortened scientific scope was substituted.
