## Interpretation and stop

The persistent E version did not translate its earlier disposable-step signal into a useful final draft: it was slower and had lower acceptance than native K+ here. The ordinary 0.96-LR control also outperformed E. This does not identify which particular learning changes caused the regressions or establish an optimal LR. No new mechanism investigation was run.

The prespecified action is to park this fixed persistent-withholding candidate. Reopening requires a separately agreed discriminating handoff; no automatic tuning, extra event, panel expansion or further probe is authorized.
