2. THREE FIXED ARMS AND EXACT ALGORITHM

E: native K+ first-error learning with current correct-prefix KL row gradients stopped; same LR as N.

The0.96 choice is the rounded median of PR48's geometry-selected alpha values (median0.9619140625), not selected using new outcomes. S is a deployable constant-LR comparator. It is NOT norm-matched to E after trajectories diverge, and it does not represent every possible LR choice.

- Reuse the qualified operation: controlled_rows = torch.where(error_role, token_loss, token_loss.detach()).
- Apply it at the existing native role hook BEFORE both the ordinary selected-loss numerator and K+'s first-error redistribution numerators. Preserve the exact native reducer/order, original selected-weight denominator, original full-response normalization where used, K+ bounded redistribution and immediate-successor priority logic.
- Preserve scalar forward values. The difference is the enabled gradient, not a new loss scale or renormalization over errors alone.
- Preserve full attention/context, all native blocks, target features, anchors, KL temperature and original selection. Stopping direct prefix-token KL gradients does not detach prefix hidden states or prevent error gradients from flowing through context representations.
- A repaired position can leave the first-error set; a position that later becomes the first error can enter it. Do not keep event-7 error labels fixed or borrow N's changing masks for E.

All arms keep the frozen target/head/embedding, trainable backbone, BF16 parameters and moments, no FP32 master weights, optimizer betas/epsilon/decay/clipping, accumulation and serving settings inherited from the authenticated native contract. Learning-rate scheduling must remain the inherited constant recipe; do not add warmup, rescaling or a scheduler.
