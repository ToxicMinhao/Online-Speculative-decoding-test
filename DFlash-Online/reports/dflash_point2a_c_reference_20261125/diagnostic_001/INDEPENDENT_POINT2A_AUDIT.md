## Limits and interpretation checks

Full gradients, directions and endpoint vectors were deliberately not retained. This audit therefore verifies scalar consistency and receipts, but cannot independently reconstruct their tensor contractions. Native logits are unavailable; the audit checks retained margins, predictions and represented assessment contributions. The 15 protected remote-input checks are authenticated retention receipts, not local rehashes of the original multi-GB inputs. Overlapping, repeatedly inspected TRAIN occurrences support neither population significance nor serving benefit.

All 11 ARS interpretation checks are covered in the machine-readable audit: Simpson’s paradox, ecological fallacy, Berkson’s paradox, collider bias, base-rate neglect, regression to the mean, survivorship bias, look-elsewhere effects, forking paths, correlation versus causation and reverse causality. Remaining design limitations are recorded rather than converted into significance or equivalence claims.
